What is Quizmoka?

Quizmoka is a YouTube project that publishes a short general-knowledge quiz every day: history, geography, science, culture and the topics of the moment. Each quiz is written with the help of AI, checked against public sources, and every video lists its sources in the description.

Channels

🇮🇹 Quizmoka Italiano

English and Spanish channels are coming soon.

How it works

  • Every morning a new quiz is written around a current topic and fact-checked on at least two sources.
  • A video is generated automatically (graphics and synthetic voice) and uploaded to the Quizmoka YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.
  • Videos are labelled as containing synthetic/AI-generated content.
